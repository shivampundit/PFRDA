AI & Machine Learning - Mock 3

Full 50 Questions · Machine Learning, NLP, LLM, Agentic AI · PFRDA Grade A Level

Syllabus Covered & Exam Scheme:

  • Machine Learning Fundamentals: Supervised, Unsupervised & Reinforcement Learning paradigms, Parametric vs Non-parametric.
  • Data Preprocessing & Feature Engineering: Imputation (MCAR, MAR, MNAR), Outlier treatment (Tukey IQR, Z-Score), Scaling (Min-Max, Standard, Robust), Encoding, SMOTE, ADASYN.
  • Model Evaluation & Overfitting: Bias-Variance Tradeoff, Confusion Matrix (Accuracy, Precision, Recall, F1, ROC-AUC), Regularization (L1 Lasso, L2 Ridge, ElasticNet), K-Fold, Stratified K-Fold.
  • Unsupervised Learning: K-Means, K-Medoids, Hierarchical (Agglomerative, Dendrogram), DBSCAN, PCA, t-SNE, UMAP.
  • Natural Language Processing (NLP): Tokenization, TF-IDF, Word Embeddings, Sentiment Analysis, VADER.
  • LLMs & Agentic AI: Transformer Architecture (Self-Attention, Multi-Head, Positional Encoding), BERT vs GPT, LoRA, QLoRA, RAG, ReAct Agent loop, Tool calling.
  • Reinforcement Learning: Markov Decision Process (MDP), Bellman Equation, Q-Learning, SARSA, PPO, DPO, RLHF.
  • Responsible AI & Governance: Explainable AI (SHAP, LIME), Fairness, DPDP Act 2023, EU AI Act risk tiers.
