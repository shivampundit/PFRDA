1. High-Frequency Quantitative Limits & Thresholds Cheat-Sheet

Regulatory Domain / Scheme Prescribed Limit / Minimum / Maximum Value Key Exam Nuance & Trap
Commercial Paper (CP) Min ₹5 Lakh (multiples of ₹5L). Tenor: 7 days to 1 year. Issuer tangible net worth ≥ ₹4 cr. Unsecured money market instrument.
Certificate of Deposit (CD) Min ₹1 Lakh (multiples of ₹1L). Tenor: 7 days to 1 year (banks). All-India FIs can issue CDs up to 3 years. Issued in demat form.
Treasury Bills (T-Bills) Min ₹10,000. Tenors: 91, 182, 364 days. State governments CANNOT issue T-bills. Zero-coupon discounted.
Cash Management Bills (CMBs) Tenor strictly < 91 days. Min ₹10,000. Non-standard tenors issued for temporary fiscal mismatches.
LRS (Liberalised Remittance) Max USD 250,000 per financial year. Only for resident individuals (not corporates/trusts). TCS applies above ₹7 Lakh.
Priority Sector Lending (PSL) 40% Domestic Banks; 75% SFBs & RRBs; 75% UCBs (by 2026). Agri 18% (10% SMF), Micro 7.5%, Weaker 12%. Shortfall → RIDF.
MUDRA (PMMY) Loan Slabs Shishu ≤ ₹50k; Kishore ₹50k–₹5L; Tarun ₹5L–₹10L; Tarun Plus ≤ ₹20L. Tarun Plus introduced in Budget 2024 for proven past repayers.
Social Stock Exchange (ZCZP) Min Issue: ₹50 Lakh; Min Application: ₹10,000. For Non-Profit Organisations; no interest, no dividend, no principal return.
P2P Lending Exposure Caps Max ₹50 Lakh across all platforms per lender; ₹10 Lakh per borrower. Escrow mechanism via bank; P2P NBFC cannot give guarantees or lend on own balance sheet.
REITs & InvITs Payouts Distribute ≥ 90% NDCF semi-annually. Min 80% in completed assets. Minimum unitholding & trading lot size now reduced to 1 unit.

2. Financial Derivatives & Option Greeks Rapid Summary

Put-Call Parity: C + K × e^(-rT) = P + S
Call Delta: 0 to +1 | Put Delta: -1 to 0 | ATM Delta ≈ 0.50
Gamma: Highest for ATM near expiry | Theta: Time decay (negative for long options)
Vega: Volatility sensitivity (highest for ATM) | Rho: Interest rate sensitivity
Strategy Name Portfolio Composition Market Outlook / When Used Max Profit & Max Loss Profile
Long Straddle Buy ATM Call + Buy ATM Put (Same Strike K & Expiry) Expects massive volatility breakout in either direction (earnings, budget). Max Profit: Unlimited; Max Loss: Total premium paid (if stock stays at K).
Long Strangle Buy OTM Call + Buy OTM Put (Different Strikes, Same Expiry) Expects extreme price movement; cheaper than straddle. Max Profit: Unlimited; Max Loss: Total premium paid (if stock stays between strikes).
Bull Call Spread Buy Lower Strike Call (K1) + Sell Higher Strike Call (K2) Moderately Bullish; lowers premium cost. Max Profit: (K2 − K1) − Net Premium; Max Loss: Net Premium.
Protective Put Long Stock + Long Put Option Bullish long-term on stock but seeks downside insurance. Synthetic long call payoff; protects below put strike price.
Covered Call Long Stock + Short OTM Call Option Neutral to mildly bullish; generates income from stock holding. Caps upside gain at strike; offers limited downside buffer equal to premium.

4. Public-Private Partnership (PPP) Models Comparison

PPP Model Financing & Capital Structure Revenue / Traffic Risk Suitability & Sector
BOT (Toll) 100% Private (Debt + Equity). Private Concessionaire bears 100% traffic risk. High-density highway corridors.
BOT (Annuity) 100% Private upfront. Government pays fixed annuity; Govt bears traffic risk. Medium-traffic roads needing private build quality.
HAM (Hybrid Annuity) 40% Govt capital grant (5 milestones) + 60% Private. NHAI collects toll; Private developer receives annuity + interest (repo-linked). Standard highway projects facing debt/traffic uncertainty.
TOT (Toll Operate Transfer) Private pays upfront lump-sum concession fee. Private collects toll & maintains for 15-30 years. Monetisation of existing operational NHAI highways.
