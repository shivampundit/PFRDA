Syllabus Covered & Exam Scheme:

  • Chapter III (Prospectus & Allotment): Sections 23–42, Public Offer, Private Placement (Sec 42), Shelf & Red Herring Prospectuses, Criminal & Civil Liabilities (Sec 34–36), PAS Rules.

  • Chapter X (Audit and Auditors): Sections 139–148, Appointment & Rotation, Resignation & Removal (Sec 140), Qualifications & Disqualifications (Sec 141), Prohibited Services (Sec 144), Fraud Reporting (Sec 143(12)), Cost Audit (Sec 148).
  • Chapter XI (Appointment & Qualifications of Directors): Sections 149–172, Board Composition, Resident & Women Directors, Independent Directors (IDs), Small Shareholders Director (SSD), DIN, Disqualifications (Sec 164), Vacation & Removal (Sec 167–169).
  • Chapter XII (Meetings of Board & its Powers): Sections 173–195, Board Meetings & Quorum (Sec 173–174), Audit/NRC/SRC Committees (Sec 177–178), Board Powers (Sec 179–180), Political/Charity Contributions (Sec 181–182), Loans to Directors (Sec 185), Inter-corporate Loans (Sec 186), Related Party Transactions (Sec 188).
  • Chapter XXVII (NCLT & NCLAT): Sections 407–434, Constitution, Qualifications of President/Chairperson/Members, Benches, Appeals to NCLAT (Sec 421) and Supreme Court (Sec 423), Timelines & Contempt.
