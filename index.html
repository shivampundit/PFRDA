🏛️ PFRDA 2025–2026 Exit & Scheme Reforms

  • Normal Exit Small Corpus (≤ ₹8 Lakh): 100% Lump Sum withdrawal permitted for both Govt & Non-Govt subscribers.
  • Mid-Tier Corpus (₹8L to ₹12L): Up to ₹6 Lakh upfront lump sum; balance via Systematic Unit Redemption (SUR ≥ 6 yrs) or annuity.
  • Corpus > ₹12 Lakh (Non-Govt): Up to 80% Lump Sum and minimum 20% Annuity (Govt remains 60% Lump Sum / 40% Annuity).
  • Unified Pension Scheme (UPS, eff. April 1, 2025): 50% assured pension (≥25 yrs service), 18.5% Govt + 10% Employee contribution, ₹10,000/mo minimum pension (≥10 yrs).
  • Multiple Scheme Framework (MSF): Non-Govt subscribers can hold multiple schemes under 1 PRAN; high-risk Category A allows up to 100% Equity.
