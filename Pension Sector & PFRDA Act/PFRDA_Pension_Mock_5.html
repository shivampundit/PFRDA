Pension Sector & PFRDA Act - Mock 5

Full 50 Questions · Updated 2024–2026 Regulations (MSF, 2025 Exits, UPS, Vatsalya) · PFRDA Grade A Level

Syllabus Covered & Exam Scheme (Updated 2024–2026):

  • PFRDA Act, 2013 & Statutory Framework: Preamble, Sections 1–54, Composition (Chairperson + max 6 members), Powers & Functions (Sec 14), Adjudication & SAT Appeals (Sec 28–38), CAG Audit (Sec 46).
  • Pension Architecture & Intermediaries: Unbundled structure (NPS Trust, CRA, PFM, Custodian, Trustee Bank, POP, ASP).
  • 2025 Exits & Withdrawals Regulations: Normal Exit ₹8 Lakh 100% lump sum; Premature Exit ₹5 Lakh threshold; ₹8L–₹12L slab (₹6L / 80% lump sum + balance SUR ≥ 6 years or annuity); Above ₹12L (80% lump sum : 20% annuity); Exit age extended to 85 years; Lien/Loan up to 25%; Partial withdrawals up to 4 times (4-year gap).
  • Multiple Scheme Framework (MSF): Multiple schemes under single PRAN, Categories A to E, Category A up to 100% equity.
  • Unified Pension Scheme (UPS): Operationalised April 1, 2025; 50% assured pension (min 25 yrs service, min 10 yrs prorated); ₹10,000 assured minimum floor; 60% family pension; 18.5% Govt contribution (8.5% pool); Lump sum on superannuation (1/10th basic+DA per 6 months).
  • NPS Vatsalya: Min ₹250 contribution; minor account opened by guardian; automatic seamless transition at age 18.
  • Taxation Framework: Section 80CCD(1), 80CCD(1B) ₹50k extra, 80CCD(2) 14% for all under New Tax Regime, Section 10(12A) 60% tax-free lump sum, Section 10(12B) 25% tax-free partial withdrawal.
  • Atal Pension Yojana (APY), Annuity Plans & Investment Basics: ₹1k–₹5k guaranteed pension slabs, Oct 1 2022 income taxpayer exclusion, Duration, Convexity, MPT, Sharpe, Treynor, Sortino ratios.
