Comprehensive Master Study Notes: Subject K — Media

1. Micro-Targeting, Digital Advertising & Consumer Behaviour in Digital Media

Micro-targeting leverages individual behavioral data, psychographic profiling (OCEAN / Big Five model), and algorithmic bidding to deliver personalized commercial and political messages.

AdTech Architecture: Programmatic Ecosystem

Component Full Form & Primary Function Role in Ecosystem
DSP Demand-Side Platform Automates ad buying across exchanges on behalf of advertisers.
SSP Supply-Side Platform Optimizes yield and manages inventory sales for digital publishers.
Ad Exchange & RTB Real-Time Bidding Auction Clears ad auctions within ~100ms as web pages load.
CDP vs DMP Customer Data Platform vs Data Management Platform CDP handles persistent 1st-party PII; DMP handles temporary anonymous 3rd-party cookie segments.

Consumer Decision-Making Models

  • AIDA Model (E. St. Elmo Lewis, 1898): Attention → Interest → Desire → Action.
  • Diffusion of Innovations (Everett Rogers, 1962): Innovators (2.5%), Early Adopters (13.5%), Early Majority (34%), Late Majority (34%), Laggards (16%).
  • Crossing the Chasm (Geoffrey Moore): The perilous transition gap between Early Adopters (visionaries) and Early Majority (pragmatists).
  • Uses & Gratifications Theory (Blumler & Katz): Audiences actively select media to satisfy cognitive, affective, social, and escapist needs.

2. Media Convergence, Extended Reality (VR/AR/MR) & Digital Divide

Henry Jenkins' Convergence Culture (2006)

Four interconnected dimensions of convergence:

  1. Technological Convergence: Digitization of words, images, sounds into universal bits across computing networks.
  2. Economic Convergence: Horizontal and vertical integration of entertainment conglomerates.
  3. Cultural Convergence: Emergence of participatory audience culture (prosumers, remix culture).
  4. Social / Organic Convergence: Multitasking across multiple screens simultaneously.

The Black Box Fallacy: The myth that one single hardware device will replace all others. In reality, devices proliferate while content converges.

Extended Reality (XR) Taxonomy

Paradigm Immersion Level Physical World Interaction Representative Hardware
Virtual Reality (VR) 100% digital immersion; complete sensory occlusion. Zero physical world visibility (opaque display). Meta Quest 3, HTC Vive, PlayStation VR.
Augmented Reality (AR) Digital overlay over physical reality. Physical world fully visible; digital annotations. Pokémon Go, Google Lens, HUDs.
Mixed Reality (MR) Physical and digital objects co-exist and interact in real-time. Occlusion mapping, spatial anchoring, 3D hand tracking. Apple Vision Pro, Microsoft HoloLens 2.

3. Entertainment Digital Platforms, Recommender Algorithms & IT Rules, 2021

Streaming Business Models

  • SVOD: Subscription Video on Demand (Netflix, Amazon Prime Video).
  • AVOD: Advertising-based Video on Demand (YouTube, MX Player).
  • TVOD: Transactional Video on Demand / Pay-per-view rental (Apple TV movie rentals).
  • FAST: Free Ad-supported Streaming TV (linear programmed streaming channels like Pluto TV).

Information Technology Rules, 2021 (Digital Media Ethics Code)

Grievance Tier Authority / Mechanism Statutory Timeline
Level I (Publisher Level) Resident Grievance Officer appointed by platform. Acknowledge in 24 hours; Resolve in 15 days.
Level II (Self-Regulating Body) Headed by retired Supreme Court / High Court judge, registered with MIB. Appeals heard from Level I decisions.
Level III (Govt Oversight) Inter-Departmental Committee (IDC) chaired by Joint Secretary, MIB. Emergency content blocking under Section 69A of IT Act.

Age-Based Classification: U (Universal), U/A 7+, U/A 13+ (Parental lock mandatory), U/A 16+ (Parental lock mandatory), A (Adults only — Age verification required).

4. Global Media Governance & Executive Negotiation Skills

Global Regulatory Paradigms

  • European Union Digital Services Act (DSA, 2024): Strict algorithmic auditability and risk assessment for Very Large Online Platforms (VLOPs, >45M users).
  • EU Digital Markets Act (DMA, 2024): Restricts anti-competitive behavior of Big Tech 'Gatekeepers' (bans self-preferencing and app store lock-in).
  • Australian News Media Bargaining Code (2021): Mandates digital platforms pay domestic news publishers for aggregated news snippets via binding arbitration.

Executive Negotiation Skills (Harvard Negotiation Project)

BATNA = Best Alternative to a Negotiated Agreement (Walk-away power)
ZOPA = Zone of Possible Agreement (Buyer Max − Seller Min Reservation Price)
Reservation Price = Absolute threshold line below/above which BATNA is pursued

Harvard Four Principles (Fisher & Ury): 1. Separate people from the problem; 2. Focus on interests, not positions; 3. Invent options for mutual gain; 4. Insist on objective criteria.

5. Audience Reception Theory, Cultural Representation, OTT Economics & Negotiation Mastery

5.1 Stuart Hall's Encoding/Decoding Model & Classical Media Effects Theories

British cultural theorist Stuart Hall (1973) overturned the passive "Hypodermic Needle / Magic Bullet" model by proposing that media texts are encoded by producers with preferred ideological meanings and actively decoded by audiences based on their socio-cultural background across three reading positions: (1) Dominant-Hegemonic Reading (accepting the producer's intended preferred meaning fully); (2) Negotiated Reading (accepting the broad framework while modifying or resisting specific local claims based on personal experience); and (3) Oppositional Reading (understanding the literal code but rejecting the ideological frame entirely).

5.2 OTT Monetization Models, Social Media Recommender Funnels & Negotiation Matrix

  • OTT Revenue Models: SVOD (Subscription Video on Demand — Netflix, Disney+); AVOD (Advertising-based Video on Demand — YouTube, JioCinema free tier); TVOD (Transactional / Pay-Per-View); FAST (Free Ad-Supported Streaming TV linear channels); and Freemium Hybrid models. Key health metrics include ARPU (Average Revenue Per User), Churn Rate, and LTV:CAC Ratio.
  • Multi-Stage Social Media Recommendation Pipeline: Modern platforms (YouTube, Instagram, X) process billions of items through a 4-stage funnel: (1) Candidate Generation (Two-Tower neural collaborative filtering narrowing millions of posts to ~1,000); (2) Fine-Grained Ranking (Deep neural networks predicting P(click), P(watch-time), P(share)); (3) Re-Ranking & Diversity Filters (freshness, creator diversity, and safety/borderline demotion); and (4) Exploration (Thompson Sampling / Contextual Bandits for cold-start content).

Theory Proponent(s) & Year Core Theoretical Principle
Hypodermic Needle Harold Lasswell (1927) Media shoots messages directly into passive audience minds with uniform, powerful effects.

Spiral of Silence Noelle-Neumann (1974) Fear of isolation causes perceived minority opinion holders to remain silent.
Third-Person Effect W. Phillips Davison (1983) People believe media influences *others* far more than *themselves*.
Decoding Position Audience Interpretation Behavior
Dominant-Hegemonic Viewer decodes the message directly in terms of the reference code in which it was produced; uncritically accepts the dominant ideology.
Negotiated Viewer acknowledges the broad legitimacy of the hegemonic message, but adapts the rules with local, personal exceptions.
Oppositional Viewer understands both literal and connotative meanings, but completely deconstructs and rejects the message within an alternative ideological framework.
Concept Definition & Strategic Value
BATNA Best Alternative to a Negotiated Agreement (Fisher & Ury). Primary source of bargaining power; determines walk-away threshold.
ZOPA Zone of Possible Agreement. Positive overlap between Buyer's Max Reservation Price and Seller's Min Reservation Price.
Reservation Price The walk-away point below/above which you will pursue your BATNA instead.
Distributive vs. Integrative Distributive: Zero-sum, fixed-pie single issue. Integrative: Win-Win, expanding the pie via trade-offs on asymmetric priorities (*Logrolling*).
Harvard 4 Principles (1) Separate people from problem; (2) Focus on interests, not positions; (3) Invent options for mutual gain; (4) Insist on objective criteria.
Thomas-Kilmann Modes (TKI) Competing (win-lose), Collaborating (win-win), Compromising (split difference), Avoiding (withdraw), Accommodating (yield).
